I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), advised by Prof. Qing Li (PolyU), Prof. Zhen Lei (CASIA) and Dr. Wenqi Fan. Before PolyU, I received my M.S. from Nanjing University and my B.S. from Shandong University.

My research goal is to build computer-use agents that keep getting better on their own, at a cost people can afford. I currently work on:

  • Computer-Use Agents. Agents that read screens and operate real software through GUIs. I have worked on scaling GUI grounding with automatically annotated functionality data (AutoGUI, ACL 2025) and on generalist GUI agents with a unified cross-platform action space (UIPro, ICCV 2025).
  • World Models for Agents. Learning how digital environments respond to actions, so an agent can predict the next screen, plan with imagined rollouts, and practice without touching the real environment.
  • Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI). Agents that propose their own tasks, generate and verify their own training signal, and use it to improve the next version of themselves, with checks that keep the loop from drifting.
  • Agentic Efficiency. Making agents cheaper and faster per task: fewer steps, tokens and model calls, lower latency, and smarter allocation of test-time compute.

These threads fit together: a world model gives an agent a cheap place to practice, self-improvement turns that practice into a better policy, and efficiency decides whether the result is usable in practice.

Earlier in my Ph.D. and during my master’s, I worked on recommender systems, graph neural networks, trustworthy AI and Bayesian optimization, with papers at KDD, SIGIR, CIKM and WWW.

selected publications

  1. ICCV
    UIPro: Unleashing Superior Interaction Capability For GUI Agents
    Hongxin Li, Jingran Su, Jingfan Chen, and 4 more authors
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 2025
    Abs arXiv Bib

    A generalist GUI agent pre-trained on about 20.6 million GUI understanding tasks to strengthen element grounding, then fine-tuned on a unified dataset that maps heterogeneous action formats into one shared action space, with strong results across GUI agent benchmarks on multiple platforms.

  2. ACL
    AutoGUI: Scaling GUI Grounding with Automatic Functionality Annotations from LLMs
    Hongxin Li, Jingfan Chen, Jingran Su, and 3 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (Volume 1: Long Papers), 2025
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Website

    An automatic pipeline that uses LLMs to annotate what UI elements do by observing how the interface changes after simulated interactions, with LLM-based rejection and verification to filter noisy labels. The resulting AutoGUI-704k dataset improves GUI grounding of vision-language models and scales with data size.

  3. ACL
    Activation Steering Decoding: Mitigating Hallucination in Large Vision-Language Models through Bidirectional Hidden State Intervention
    Jingran Su, Jingfan Chen, Hongxin Li, and 3 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (Volume 1: Long Papers), 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML PDF

    A training-free decoding method that identifies hallucination-related directions in the hidden states of large vision-language models from a small calibration set and steers generation away from them with contrastive decoding.
